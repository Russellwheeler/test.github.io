The story

Wiggle designed this app between naps on the blue blanket. His brief was simple: find anything on the internet, be a little bit cheeky, and never ask you to get off the sofa. The tuxedo colour scheme was non-negotiable, and so was the eyebrow. He's also a proud vegetarian, so every recipe search starts veggie.

He's now fully retired from duty, but his name stays on the door.

The important bit ❤️
