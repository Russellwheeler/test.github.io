Waiting for pedal strokes

Pedal at a steady 80–90 rpm with moderate effort. You'll get a result after about 6 strokes.

How to get an accurate reading
  1. Camera side-on: exactly at 90° to the bike, at about hip height, level, and not tilted. Viewing from an angle makes the knee look more bent or straighter than it is.
  2. Whole leg in view from hip to foot, with good light. Tight shorts or leggings work best.
  3. Ride in your normal shoes, on the hoods, at a steady effort. Measure the leg nearest the camera.
  4. For a sharper result, record a slow-motion clip with the phone camera app and use Analyse video.
  • Knee bend at bottom is how far the knee is still bent when the pedal is at its lowest point (0° = dead straight). Dynamic bike-fit guidelines usually aim for about 30–40°. Below that, the saddle is likely too high. Above it, the saddle is likely too low.
  • Change the saddle height in small steps (3–5 mm), ride a few minutes, and measure again.
  • Pain, numbness, or a big difference between your legs is a job for a professional bike fitter or physio. This tool is only a guide.
